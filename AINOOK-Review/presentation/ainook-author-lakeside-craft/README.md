在 AINOOK 项目根目录运行独立预览。

```powershell
python outputs/prototypes/ainook-author-lakeside/serve.py --port 7895 --preview-dir outputs/prototypes/ainook-author-lakeside-craft
```

本版共用湖边小屋的模板和互动逻辑。`art/lakeside.webp` 是新的日夜主图，`room.css` 将四处点击范围和反馈效果对准新构图。

Same subtle slightly elevated three-quarter camera, same scale, exact furniture and scenery placement. No text, logos or panel frames.

Reference the observation, warmth and stillness of hand-painted Japanese animated feature backgrounds with the material precision of a premium hand-built stop-motion set. Draw realistic, sharply resolved surfaces with fine wood grain aligned board by board, canvas weave and stitched seams, dulled brass touches, hand-glazed earthenware, and beveled stone edges. Show crisp intentional silhouettes and perfectly sensible joints, props cast legible shadows, no painterly fraying around the whole silhouette. Precise contours and physically grounded depth. Lots of thoughtful scale clues. Restrained painterly handling on landscape and color; not a chalky poster, watercolor vignette, isolated furniture showroom, plastic 3D game prop, pixel art, blurry background wash, or photographic live-action. Calm inhabited grown-up workspace and no visible characters. Beautiful composition and elegant natural materials.
