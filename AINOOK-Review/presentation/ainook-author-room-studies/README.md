# AINOOK 作者小屋日夜方案

在 AINOOK 项目根目录运行独立预览。

```powershell
python outputs/prototypes/ainook-author-room-studies/serve.py
```

打开 [小屋预览](http://127.0.0.1:7893/author)，用左侧文字切换三个房间，用网站原有的日夜开关查看两种光线。可与 [上一版](http://127.0.0.1:7892/author) 同时访问。关闭预览进程即可退出本版。

每个房间的插画把白天和黑夜叠在一张透明的 WebP 中。浏览器根据网站主题展示对应半张。物件的点击范围和反馈效果位置在 `rooms.json` 中，可分别调整。每次切换方案会以对应房间的初始状态开始，日夜切换会保留当前房间的互动。

## 插画来源

三套日夜画面由 ImageGen 生成。每次调用沿用公共提示并追加对应房间的设计描述。

```text
Create ONE transparent RGBA, vertical 2:3 asset, a day/night paired sprite sheet for an interactive room on the AINOOK website. The canvas is divided into TWO EXACT EQUAL ROWS, no border or text. The TOP 50% shows the daytime edition of the room below; the BOTTOM 50% shows the IDENTICAL room at night. Think of exporting two lighting passes of the exact same 3D scene, with frame, camera, floor geometry, furniture placements and silhouettes perfectly registered relative to each half. Make the room occupy about 93% width and 84% height of EACH half, with matched clear transparent margins especially along the central horizontal seam. Everything outside the floor base and walls is fully transparent. Each half is a handsome professional contemporary design miniature, open-front 2.5D cutaway with a shallow beveled floor plinth and rear wall plus one short return wall. Fit the complete miniature in its half; no chopping off the floor. Show the scene almost straight-on and gently above the desk height, enough perspective to feel dimensional, no wide-angle distortion. Matte, softly textured crafted materials, a restrained contemporary editorial illustration / architectural showroom rendering. Adult and lived-in. No retro or antique electronics, no dusty knickknacks, no pixel art, no cartoon people, no interface, no caption, no callout, no crosshair, no watermark. Architecture and proportions are important. Selective beautiful material details and generous open space.
The NIGHT panel must look intentionally relit, not tinted all-over or merely darker. Show dusk/blue-hour/city-night through the real window, subdued bluish moonlight, observed local warm lighting around the place occupants would leave a light on, cool shadows, preserved softly legible furniture; one of the individual target lamps remains OFF in BOTH panels, so the frontend can turn on that object. Do not bake in any shine or effect on computer screens or clickable objects not intended to be lit. DAY panel has textured directional daylight, subtle shadows and clearly different real exterior scene.

VARIANT A: light contemporary creative studio, with a broad landscape picture window with soft rounded corners to the LAYOUT LEFT, an asymmetrical pale ash built-in floating workspace and a slender laptop slightly LEFT of center, laptop screen OFF. Pale periwinkle and slate-blue architecture, smoked light grey and creamy pale oak, a precise single cobalt-blue color accent to a chair or chair cushion; no pink. Room should feel like an excellent current independent tech studio, no retro tech. Important clickable objects well apart with completely unobstructed silhouettes: (1) unobtrusive matte off-white conical small table lamp, OFF, on the desk's FAR LEFT; (2) slim contemporary open laptop, dark screen, near the CENTER; (3) simple transparent carafe and a low glass with water on a visually distinct narrow shelf or table at the FAR RIGHT. Add a pinboard with a couple abstract off-white scraps or a sculptural digital clock, no text. Day city soft horizon and white clouds through window, the same view with a handful of twilit skyline lights at night; gentle under-shelf wall wash at night.

VARIANT B: a modern apartment winter garden for hobbies, compact architectural green room and reading nook. Pale sage walls, lime-washed cream surfaces, moss-green key accents, muted coral and travertine, trimmed clean rectangular glazing, fresh and quiet. Not a Victorian greenhouse. Plenty of margins around distinct objects. Focus on one beautiful bench seat with green linen, low round mineral side table and paperback, terracotta and glaze pots, modern flat folded materials; designer home next to a city park. Important clickable objects spread out: (1) one medium rubber plant floor pot on the FAR LEFT with clearly defined leaves, (2) an OPEN slim paperback sitting flat on the low table in the CENTER with blank decorative markings only, (3) a contemporary long-stem floor lamp with soft-white spherical glass globe on the FAR RIGHT, turned OFF both times. A few less prominent plants or details can occupy edges. Day window shows layered green trees and washed sky; night window shows silhouettes of trees against an easy-to-read blue dusk, maybe a point of outdoor path light, hidden warm light washing the back wall and no light coming from the globe until the visitor clicks.

VARIANT C: warm contemporary city living room and listening space, more sculptural than a normal desk scene. Cream-pink limewashed interior, warm sand floor, terracotta and tomato accents, a contemporary low modular fabric sofa, a clean thick travertine block table, slender brushed metal, some aubergine. A single dramatic tall skylight or vertical window on the LEFT with deep architectural reveal. Polished editorial small modern apartment spa-like room, no vintage or midcentury props. Important clickable objects well apart: (1) a small sculptural matte mushroom or frosted orange dome accent lamp OFF on its pedestal LOW LEFT, (2) a ceramic tea cup on the central low table, (3) a contemporary compact black rectangular speaker with delicate fabric texture on the FAR RIGHT cabinet, no vintage record player. Crisp changing atmosphere across the SAME cuts. Day luminous soft peach direct sun through the tall window, night navy outside with a street-light glint and a visually distinct soft warm reflected ceiling or wall light.
```
